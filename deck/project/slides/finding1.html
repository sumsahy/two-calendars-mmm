Cells: P(15% shift raises sales), spend-share prior; under 0.80 = not supported. Mean absolute change vs oracle: corrected 0.03, 4-4-5 0.09, week-start 0.10 (noise about 0.02).

Mechanism: week-start moves spend into the month a week starts, ahead of when it airs and works. TV looks less tied to sales, its marginal ROI drops (0.50 vs 0.69 on perfect data) and digital, which partly moves with TV, picks up the credit (1.31 vs 1.01). The 4-4-5 rollup understates TV's share (9.1% vs 11.6%) and adds noise that drags P down by up to 0.35 in some histories: noise hides signal rather than faking it. Equal-prior sensitivity: week-start +0.07 vs oracle and a confident Holds in the world where the shift hurts.
