The Two Calendars: does the TV calendar mismatch change the 15% TV-to-digital call?

Synthetic diabetes-brand data with a known true effect per channel. TV arrives in Monday-to-Sunday media weeks on a 4-4-5 calendar; digital, email and sales arrive in calendar months. Four ways of building monthly TV are fitted with the same Bayesian MMM and scored against the truth.

Verdict under the pre-registered rule

Holds if P(the 15% shift raises sales) ≥ 0.80. Locked in git before any model was fitted.
